# Brief — Zedu Tower website

**Product.** Zedu Tower is a spatial view of a Zedu (Slack-like) workspace: teams are floors, channels are rooms, and people are where their presence says they are. Today it is a clickable prototype with sample data at `/app/`.

**Audience.** Nexprove's own team and early Zedu customers: team leads and founders of 10–100 person companies who already live in chat and feel they've lost the sense of "who's around". Secondary: designers and engineers reviewing the prototype.

**Primary task.** Understand the idea in one screen, then open the prototype. Secondary: read the guide to test it properly.

**Trust posture.** Converts on curiosity, not credibility. There is no pricing or sign-up yet; the site must make people *want to look inside*.

**Interaction budget.** Tier 3 of 4: one signature interactive moment (the focus window over a living tower), a few product-driven micro-scenes, no WebGL, no heavy libraries. Must stay smooth on a mid-range laptop and degrade to static on reduced motion.

**Constraints.** Max 3 pages. Static hosting on Vercel. Content must be in the initial HTML (link unfurlers and crawlers). Reuse the product's typefaces. Light and dark.

**Success.** A visitor can say "it's our chat, as an office building" within 10 seconds, and clicks *Open the prototype*.

**Out of scope.** Pricing, accounts, waitlist backend, blog.
