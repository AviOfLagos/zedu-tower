# Zedu Tower

A clickable prototype that turns a Zedu workspace into an office building: teams are floors, channels are rooms, people are where their presence says they are. Runs entirely in the browser on sample data.

- **App:** `index.html` (built from `src/app.html`)
- **Guide:** `docs.html` explains the mapping, statuses, notifications and the built-in test scenarios

## Develop

Edit `src/app.html`, then run `sh build.sh` to regenerate `index.html`. No dependencies.
