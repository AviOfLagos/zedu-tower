Got questions?Here's what people ask first.

Is this connected to our Zedu workspace?

Not yet. The prototype runs on sample data: 42 people across 10 floors. The next step is generating floors from your groups and rooms from your channels.

Can people see what I'm doing?

They see your presence: active, away, do not disturb, offsite or offline. Activity like "On a call with Kemi" shows only if you share it. You can keep it to presence only.

What about private channels?

If you're a member, the room looks normal. If not, it's frosted: you can see it's in use, but not who's inside or what's said.

Does it replace the chat?

No. Messages still land in Zedu instantly. The building is a way to see who's around and pick the right way to reach them.

Does it work on my phone?

The office view is built for computers and tablets. On a phone, you keep using the regular Zedu app.

How do I test it properly?

Open the prototype and press Tests. Twenty-one scripted scenarios play out in front of you and tick off what they check. The guide explains each one.
